Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding queue estimations, emergency priority rules, and appointment scheduling.

How does the Estimated Wait Time calculation work?

Our smart wait-time estimator dynamically calculates wait duration based on the active patient count, historical department speed trends, and individual doctor consultation durations. If an emergency triggers, other estimated times will update automatically.

What happens if a doctor gets called for an emergency?

Emergency patients bypass standard scheduling sequences. The doctor triggers an Emergency State from their panel, slotting that case immediately into the next spot. This ensures critical care is delivered instantly without manual rescheduling.

Can I track my live position if I step out of the hospital?

Yes. MediQueue is fully mobile-responsive and functions as a web application. Once checked in, you can monitor the live feed, check doctor status, and watch your position advance directly from your smartphone browser.

How do I cancel or reschedule my place in the queue?

You can use the AI assistant widget at the bottom right to request a status change, or simply report to the desk where administrators can shift or cancel your ticket sequence with a single click in their panels.
